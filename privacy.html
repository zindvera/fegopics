Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to ScrollStrike. ScrollStrike is built and maintained under FegoPics, an independent developer profile run by a solopreneur. We believe privacy should be simple, clear, and transparent. We only handle the minimum data needed to provide your plugin files and verify purchases.

1. Zero Tracking on Your Website

  • Your readers are never tracked: When visitors read your blog posts and view your highlighted links, all animations and previews run locally inside their web browser and your own WordPress database.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts: We do not collect reader data, visitor IP addresses, or link click history from your site.

2. Our Two Plugin Builds

To keep things simple, ScrollStrike comes in two versions:

A. Subscription Build (Free & Recurring Plans)

  • License Verification: When you enter a recurring license key (Monthly, 6 Months, or 1 Year), the plugin connects to Gumroad's official API strictly to verify whether your key is active and matches your plan.
  • Payments: All payments are processed securely by Gumroad. We never see or store your credit card or billing details.

B. Lifetime PRO Build (One-Time Payment & AppSumo)

  • Completely Standalone: This version is built for lifetime license holders (purchased either as a one-time payment on Gumroad or via AppSumo).
  • Zero External API Calls: This build has no license check code, no Gumroad API calls, and no pings to any server. Once installed, it runs 100% independently forever.

3. Code Redemption (redeem.html)

  • If you purchase through AppSumo, you receive a redemption code.
  • You enter your code on redeem.html to verify it and immediately receive your direct download link for the Lifetime PRO .zip file.
  • We do not ask for unnecessary personal details or require lengthy account registration forms.

4. Support Emails

  • When you email us at support@fegopics.com, we only use your email address and message to help answer your questions or solve technical issues.
  • We never sell your email, share your data, or send you unsolicited marketing spam newsletters.
